# MTG Roguelite — Player Cheat Sheet

> Compact table reference for playing an MTGR run. Use the Full Rules for edge cases and complete subsystem text.

## 1 — Build the Run Deck

- Generate **5 random legal Commanders** and choose 1.
- Each player normally starts with **4 Commander Mulligans**.
- Generate a personal **100-card pool** in the starting Commander's color identity, then draft **22 cards** from that pool.
- Initial deckbuilding uses **16 lands total** unless another effect changes that number.
- The base minimum is **40 total cards: 1 Commander + at least 39 cards in the library**.
- Each player normally gets **1 Free Choice Card**.
- A Free Choice Card may be outside Commander color identity, but cannot be **Sol Ring** or a **Gamechanger** unless another effect allows it.
- Scryfall-stamped cards cannot be traded or captured. Follow Deckbuilding for their winning-combo and infinite-loop restrictions.
- A two-card combo that wins using only those two cards is banned.
- Infinite loops involving two or fewer cards, or any Scryfalled card, are limited to one iteration per turn. Created objects cannot restart that loop that turn, and extra turns from it cannot chain the same loop to generate further extra turns. See Deckbuilding.

## 2 — Before Encounter 1

Resolve in order:

1. Starting **Event** — it must be classified Good.
2. **Trinket**.

Ticket Trinkets are additional to the voted party Trinket. They are usable by the party and tied to their Ticket owners; follow the Trinket System for removal after departure.

After deckbuilding and before Encounter 1, players have a one-time seat-swap window. After Encounter 1 begins, seat swapping is no longer allowed unless another active effect specifically permits it.

## 3 — Player Health

- Each player's max health starts at **20** unless an effect explicitly changes **Max HP/Life**.
- Healing restores current life only up to the current max.
- "Gain life" and "lose life" change current life only unless the effect explicitly changes Max HP/Life.

## 4 — Shared Turn / Combat

### Party Turn

The party shares turn timing as described in Turn Structure; each player retains control of their own cards and permanents.

- One shared Untap, Upkeep, and Draw.
- Shared precombat main, combat, postcombat main, end, and cleanup.
- All party attackers are declared simultaneously.
- The Host blocks after the full attack declaration.
- Controller-specific attack wording is checked separately for each player. Combat-wide wording can apply across allied attackers.

### Host Turn

- The Host takes a normal turn.
- The Host chooses attack targets.
- Adjacent players may block for each other when an adjacent ally is attacked.
- Legal adjacent blockers count toward blocking requirements such as Menace.

## 5 — Death

- Death is **not permanent**.
- Dead players return in future encounters.
- There is **no XP penalty** for dying.

## 6 — After an Encounter

### If the Party Wins

Resolve all three rewards immediately after victory:

- **XP**
- **Cashout**
- **Loot Pool**

They may be resolved in any order. After all three are complete, choose **Town or Stay Out**, except before the Crypt, when Town is mandatory.

### If the Party Loses

A loss advances the scheduled encounter number. Do not replay the encounter.

1. Skip XP, Cashout, and Loot Pool.
2. Still choose **Town or Stay Out**, except before the Crypt, when Town is mandatory.
3. Reset all players to full HP before the next encounter.
4. Event resolution continues normally except before the Crypt, when no Events are drawn.

Missing the encounter rewards is the penalty for the failed encounter.

## 7 — Town or Stay Out

### Town

Reveal the Town Traveler from the Travelers bag; Crossroads Cart reveals two and the party chooses one.

1. Fully heal all players.
2. Use Town buildings.
3. Resolve Event(s).

### Stay Out

1. Fully heal all players to current max HP.
2. Gain **10 XP per player**, plus **+5 XP for each consecutive Stay Out**.
3. Gain **2 Mystery Packs per player**.
4. Resolve a Supply Drop.
5. Resolve a Wanderer.
6. Resolve **2 Events**, plus **+1 Event for each consecutive Stay Out**.
7. Begin the next encounter.

Returning to **Town resets the Stay Out streak**. Players cannot use Town buildings while Staying Out.

## 8 — Event Resolution

For each Event:

1. Draw or roll a random Event.
2. Resolve its immediate instructions.
3. Record any continuing, delayed, Town, next-encounter, or run-long effect.
4. Repeat if multiple Events are required.

An Event does not have to stop affecting the run before another Event can be resolved. The printed Event card controls its specific effect and duration.

## 9 — The Crypt

After the final scheduled encounter, win or lose, Town is mandatory. Stay Out is unavailable. Resolve Town normally, then face the Crypt without drawing Events.

- **No Affixes** are applied.
- The **Host takes the first turn**.
- **No Events** are resolved.
