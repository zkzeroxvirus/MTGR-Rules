# Host Commands playtest

Status: opt-in playtest. This is not a replacement for published Host Authority
rules until promoted. Choose this package or legacy Not Today/free Disallow,
never both. Keep Doom, passive scaling, Demonic Persistence, and Arcane Suppression.

## Progression and resources

Use the Host Profile on the same Steam account as the Player Profile. A verified
eligible run grants 100 HXP alongside the existing Host Essence reward, once.
Kills, wins, Command uses, and review scores do not grant additional HXP.

Ranks 1–10 begin at 0, 100, 250, 450, 700, 1000, 1350, 1750, 2200, and 2700 HXP.
Ranks 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, and 10 grant one Talent Point each. Rank does not increase
damage, life, mana, Command slots, or Authority.

The extra-encounter variant grants +1 starting Authority. Lock the player count,
mode, Talents, and loadout before Encounter 1. Each equipped Command costs
1 Authority and may be committed once per encounter. Encounter changes restore
Command uses, not Authority. Town and Crypt do not refill Authority. Seats joining
or leaving do not change locked resources. Talents and loadout cannot change
during a run or its pending review.

## Resolution and counterplay

When the Host commits an ordinary Command with legal timing, put its
Host-controlled triggered ability on the stack. Players may respond and counter
that ability where their cards allow it. These are system abilities, not abilities
of Host permanents; Twice the Power does not copy them. Absolute Command —
Disallow instead casts its defined normal spell and follows spell rules and
applicable affix interactions.

Authority and the encounter use are spent on commitment. There is no refund if
the Command is countered, redirected, or fails to resolve because targets become
illegal. The table adjudicates timing, target legality, costs, and resolution;
the UI records resources, not the Magic stack.

## Starter Commands

- **Absolute Command — Disallow:** When you could cast an instant, cast a copy of
  Disallow without paying its mana cost: counter target spell, activated ability,
  or triggered ability. This is a normal spell.
- **Demonic Ward:** When you have priority, target nonland permanent you control
  gains either hexproof or indestructible until end of turn. Choose on resolution.
- **Twisted Targeting:** When an opponent's spell or ability has exactly one
  target and targets you or a permanent you control, change its target to another
  legal target. Do not redirect unrelated value effects.
- **Break the Charge:** During combat before combat damage, prevent all combat
  damage one target attacking creature would deal this combat. Attack triggers
  are unaffected.

## Branch Commands

Each Tier I Talent requires Rank 2. Each Tier II Talent requires Rank 4 and its
branch's Tier I Talent. Each costs one Talent Point.

### Dominion — stack control

- **Seal the Combo (I):** When you have priority, counter target activated or
  triggered ability. Later abilities from that source are unaffected. Mana
  abilities do not use the stack.
- **Answer the Answer (II):** When a spell targets a spell or ability you control,
  counter that spell.

### Torment — pressure

- **Cruel Choice (I):** When a player casts their second spell of the turn,
  counter that spell unless its controller pays 5 life. If the spell has left the
  stack, no payment is required.
- **Forced Exposure (II):** During your main phase with an empty stack, target
  player reveals their hand. Choose a nonland card name from it. The next spell
  that player casts with that name costs {2} more. This expires at the end of
  their next turn.

### Control — board and graveyard control

- **Strip the Shield (I):** When you have priority, choose a player's nonland
  permanent on resolution. It loses hexproof and indestructible until end of
  turn. The choice does not target and does not itself remove the permanent.
- **Grave Interdict (II):** When you have priority, exile one target card from an
  opponent's graveyard. Other cards and graveyard actions are unaffected.

Tier III Last Word, Marked for Death, and Divide and Conquer are inactive; they
cannot be learned or equipped. Six active Talents can all be learned at Rank 10.
Do not claim lasting specialization until capstones are approved; loadout slots
still limit encounter options.

## Balance review

Use the declared Commander Bracket as intent/context, not an automatic numeric
multiplier. Do not classify every precon as Bracket 2 or assume MTGR's 40-card
drafted player decks have an automatically known bracket. Higher bracket selection
does not grant Authority.

Review actual deck archetype, affixes and tiers, selected season, Doom count, and
party progression before locking. Doom already provides large draws, tutors,
wipes, protection, and other swings. Commands should be tactical answers, not
duplicate engines or broad lockdowns. Pay particular attention to Dominion denial
chains, Forced Exposure plus Arcane Suppression, and protecting affix-enhanced
threats. Preserve the prohibited Twice the Power + Annihilator 1 pairing.

Collect encounter length, denial chains, party ability to execute its plans,
Authority left, actual Commands used, deck Bracket, and encounter/affix context.
Win rate alone does not establish balance. No Command-use rewards.
