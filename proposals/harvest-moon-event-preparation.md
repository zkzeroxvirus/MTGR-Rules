# Harvest Moon prepared content

Harvest Moon remains inactive. The user authorized preparation and integration, not activation or announcements.

The final Platform inventory contains 93 physical Event cards (83 designs), 15 Doom cards, eight random Trinkets, two Nylea-only Trinkets, two Travelers, three Wanderers and twelve encounter decks. Pumpkin Harvest is Event — Neutral. Bow of Nylea is the regular Magic card. Doom metadata, including BloodRush, has been transcribed from artwork and retained in the Platform source metadata overlay.

Confirmed rules: Cornfields is once per Town per player; optional Crypt choices are decided by the party and cannot override compulsory encounters; Harvest Festival covers all packs; Dead Leaf follows normal one-turn phasing; Full Moon covers all double-faced cards; Legendary Shovel has no benefit at four or more growth counters; Reaper XP is per player.

Reap What You Sow is registered in PROGRESSION-CONTRACTS.json with its dedicated canonical source at rules/progression/crypt-buffs/reap-what-you-sow.md. Platform uses the standard one-slot, nonrepeatable Crypt-buff metadata and Beat Harvest Moon unlock. Registration does not activate the event or award ownership.

Platform's docs/harvest-moon-release.md describes Profile actions, visitor tribute and rewards, Host controls, tabletop resolution boundaries, validation and the remaining live TTS smoke test. Both repositories must be released together when activation is authorized.
