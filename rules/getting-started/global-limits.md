# **⚖️ GLOBAL LIMITS**

Each player must have:  
 • 1 Commander  
 • A Deck at or above their **modified deck minimum**, following [Deck Minimum and Town Removals](deck-minimum.md), including its Town restoration deadline

Each player may have:  
 • An **unlimited-size Sideboard**  
 • 4 Buff Slots (Includes Crypt Buffs, Achievements, Tickets and Captures.)  
 • 1 Partner  
 • 1 Background  
 • 1 Conspiracy  
 • 1 Vanguard  
 • 1 Emblem  
 • 1 Trinket tied to their Ticket, in addition to the normal voted party Trinket
 • 1 Companion
