# **🎲 Road Gambler**

*Once per Wanderer encounter.*

Pay 5 XP.

Sacrifice a card from your deck.
Roll a d6.

Choose a card type.
Gain a random card of that type that shares a color with the sacrificed card, with rarity based on the roll:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | Random Common |
| 3–4 | Random Uncommon |
| 5 | Random Rare |
| 6 | Random Mythic |
